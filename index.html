Frequently Asked Questions

Your questions, answered.

What events do you cater for?

Weddings, receptions, engagements, birthdays, anniversaries, religious and corporate events.

What is the minimum guest count?

Usually from 50 guests; call us for smaller gatherings.

What is the cost per plate?

It depends on the menu and guest count. We provide a customised quote.

Can I customise the menu?

Yes, every menu is designed around your preferences.

Are veg and non-veg both available?

Both, with separate kitchen arrangements.

Do you provide live counters?

Yes: chaat, tandoor, jalebi, dosa and pasta counters.

How early should I book?

At least 2 to 3 months ahead in wedding season.

Do you serve other cities?

Bulandshahr, Kanpur, Varanasi, Prayagraj, Agra and across Uttar Pradesh.
